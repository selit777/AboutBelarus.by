MAIN

This is Belarus, our country. Our head of state is president – A. G. Lukashenko.
Our national symbols are cornflower, european bison and white stork. We have emblem, anthem and flag.
The population of Belarus is about 9.5 million. Our capital is Minsk. The population of Minsk is about 2 million.
We have many popular cities. For example, Vitebsk, Mogilev, Brest and other.
We very love Belarus.
